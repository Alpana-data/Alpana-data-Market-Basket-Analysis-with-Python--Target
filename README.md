# Overview:
Target : Leading Global e-commerse platform 
Huge customer & product data
Goal: Improve personalization & Sales

# Objective:
Analyze Customer behaviour and  Product Recommendation Report Apply market Basket Analysis  techniques. Perform clustering &amp; segmentation. Generate actionable insights for personalized product recommendations, promotional strategies, and inventory optimization. 

# Tools Used:
Python, Python libraries: Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib, Scikit-Learn

# Dataset: 
Customer Demographics, Purchase Frequency, Browsing behaviour, Satisfaction & Reviews , Recommendation helpfulness 

# Tasks Performed:
Data Cleaning
Descriptive Analysis
Customer Behaviour Analysis
Customer Segmentation
Clustering (K-means)

# Key Finding and Insights:
High satisfaction = higher purchases
High At-Risk Customers
Majority users browse Multiple times a day
Recommendations influence decisions: Customers don’t find recommendations significantly helpful → low correlation
Certain products are frequently bought together: Beauty & personal care and Clothing & Fashion
The analysis indicates that customer trust in personalized recommendations remains relatively consistent across different levels of engagement.
